A stained or sagging ceiling means water is above it. Catch the drip, keep clear of the sag and call us.

Emergency ceiling water damage repair in Rockville Centre starts when you see a brown ring, a bubble in the paint or water dripping from a light fixture. The ceiling is only where the water finally showed itself. The source may be a bathroom above, a supply line in the joists or a leak in the roof.

Call our 24/7 line, move furniture from under the stain and place a bucket where it drips. If the ceiling is bowing, do not stand beneath it.

When a leaking ceiling is an emergency

A ceiling that is dripping, bulging or sagging is an emergency. Wet drywall and plaster are heavy, and a bulge can fail without much warning.

A small ring that does not grow can wait for a daytime visit, but it still needs a cause. Old stains that look dry may hide a slow leak that returns every time it rains or someone takes a shower.

Safety first: if water is coming through a light fixture or ceiling fan, stay away and turn off the breaker only if you can reach it dry. Poke a small hole to relieve a bulge only if you can do it from a safe distance into a bucket.

Finding where the water starts

We trace the water with a moisture meter and thermal camera. A ceiling stain right under a bathroom usually means a supply line, drain or tub seal. A stain along an exterior wall after rain points to the roof, flashing or gutters.

This page also covers roof leaks that reach the ceiling. We dry and repair the interior, while a roofer fixes the roof itself, and the two jobs should be timed together so the new ceiling is not wet by the next storm.

How the ceiling is repaired

  1. Stop the water at its source or place a temporary cover over the leak.
  2. Remove wet or sagging drywall, plaster or insulation.
  3. Dry the joists and cavity with air movers and a dehumidifier.
  4. Confirm readings are normal before any new board is hung.
  5. Patch, texture and prime the ceiling, then paint.

Plaster ceilings in older homes need a different approach than drywall, and the right repair depends on how much of it is detached. A wet attic or insulation layer above the ceiling may also need drying, similar to the steps in wall and drywall water damage repair.

Ceiling leaks in Rockville Centre homes

Many houses in Rockville Centre have two stories with a bathroom directly above a first-floor ceiling. A failed supply line or toilet seal on the upper floor is a common cause, so check the wet area above before blaming the roof.

Rockville Centre residential repairs generally need a permit from the building department, and roofing contractors in Nassau County need a Home Improvement License. Ask a roofer for the license number before you sign.

Standards behind the work

The industry reference for this kind of work is ANSI/IICRC S500, the standard for professional water damage restoration. Whoever you hire, ask how it applies to your job and ask to see the moisture readings that show the structure is dry.
