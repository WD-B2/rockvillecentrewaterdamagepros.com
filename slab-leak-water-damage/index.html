Emergency slab leak water damage in Rockville Centre is about the water a hidden line has already put into your floor. A leak under or in a concrete slab can run for weeks before the signs show, and by then the flooring above it may be soaked.

Close the main valve to confirm the water stops, then call our 24/7 line. A plumber finds and repairs the line. We dry the structure and explain what the readings mean.

Signs of a leak under the floor

The signs are subtle. You may hear water running when every tap is off, feel a warm patch on a floor, see a damp spot on tile or carpet, or notice your water bill jump with no change in use.

A few simple checks narrow it down. If the water meter keeps spinning with everything off, or the reading changes overnight, there is a leak somewhere between the meter and the fixtures.

Safety first: if water is coming up through the floor near outlets or an electrical panel, stay out and call 911 or the utility. Close the main valve and turn off power to the area only if you can do it from dry ground.

What a slab leak does to a house

Water under a slab moves through cracks and joints and rises into flooring, baseboards and the bottom of walls. It can also wash soil away from the slab, which is a structural concern for a plumber and an engineer to judge.

Carpet, wood and laminate over a slab leak often swell or buckle. Tile may crack or grout may stain. A moisture meter reads how far the water traveled.

How this fits a Rockville Centre home

Many houses in the village have basements, so slab-on-grade floors are less common than in warmer states. You will find them in additions, garages and some ranch-style homes, and a leak in one of those areas follows this pattern.

If your water bill jumped and you have not found a surface leak, read appliance leak water damage for the checks that come before assuming it is under the floor.

How we handle the drying

  1. Confirm moisture with meters and thermal imaging after the plumber has located the leak.
  2. Extract any water that came up through the floor.
  3. Lift or remove flooring that cannot dry in place.
  4. Dry the slab surface and the wall bottoms with air movers and dehumidification.
  5. Check readings until the structure is back to normal.

Plumbers use leak detection tools to find the break and may tunnel, break the slab or reroute the line. Drying runs alongside that work. See structural drying for how readings guide the schedule.
