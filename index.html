The process

How an emergency water damage call goes

  1. You call

    The 24/7 line is answered by a person who asks about the source and the safety of the home.

  2. Stop the source

    We guide you to the shutoff, or tell you when to wait for a plumber.

  3. Extract and dry

    Standing water comes out, then air movers and dehumidifiers dry the structure.

  4. Check and finish

    Moisture readings show when the home is dry and what repair remains.
