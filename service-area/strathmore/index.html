What goes wrong in older Tudor and Colonial homes

Older houses can still carry original supply lines, cast iron drain stacks and plaster walls. A pinhole leak in a wall may run for days before a stain shows on the ceiling below.

Plaster and lath hold water differently than modern drywall, so a wet wall needs moisture readings before anyone decides what to cut. Our guide to wall and drywall water damage explains how that decision is made.

Basements are the other common call. After a long rain, groundwater can seep through a foundation wall or rise through the floor, and basement water damage cleanup covers the steps from pumping to drying.

Ceilings suffer when a bathroom upstairs leaks. For that situation see ceiling water damage repair, which explains why a sagging ceiling should be handled with care.

Heavy rain and Long Beach Road

Long Beach Road runs along the west edge of Strathmore. Local reporting from the Long Island Herald described a storm that dropped an estimated nine inches of rain in six hours, and parts of Long Beach Road, Hempstead Avenue, Lakeview Avenue and Peninsula Boulevard were knee deep in water.

That report concerned roads, and we have no record of any particular Strathmore home flooding in it. It does show how fast the village can fill with water in a cloudburst, which is why a sump pump or a floor drain deserves a test before the storm.

The village has said its crews clear drains ahead of hurricane season. You can help by keeping the storm drain in front of your house free of leaves and debris.

If water is spreading in your Strathmore home

These safety steps cost nothing and help most in the first hour. Do them only if it is safe to reach the spot.

  • Close the main water valve if the water comes from a pipe, a fixture or an appliance.
  • Stay out of any room where water touches outlets, cords or the electrical panel.
  • Lift small valuables, rugs and electronics to a dry shelf.
  • Photograph the water line on walls and the damaged items before you move them.
  • Do not use a household vacuum on standing water.

Permits and village contacts

Rockville Centre is an incorporated village, so building questions go to the village Building Department at 110 Maple Avenue. The flood insurance study and flood maps for the village are kept on file at that office.

If a repair after water damage will involve structural, plumbing or electrical work, ask the Building Department what permit applies before work begins. We dry and clean the structure, and licensed trades handle permitted repair work.
