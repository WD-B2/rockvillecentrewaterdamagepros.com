If water is still spreading

These steps cost nothing and help most in the first hour. Do them before the crew arrives, but only if it is safe.

  • Close the main water valve if the source is a pipe or fixture.
  • Turn off power to wet rooms only if the panel is dry and you can reach it.
  • Move small valuables and electronics to a dry shelf.
  • Photograph the water line on walls and floors before anything is moved.
  • Keep children and pets out of standing water.
