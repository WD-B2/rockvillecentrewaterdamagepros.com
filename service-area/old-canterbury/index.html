About Old Canterbury

Real estate listings describe Old Canterbury as an established section of the village with tree-lined streets and stately homes. Several listings place Banbury Road, Huber Court, Vanderveer Court and Cash Lane inside it.

Those descriptions come from property listings and not from a village boundary map. If your street is not listed here, call and we will confirm coverage, because we serve all of Rockville Centre.

Listings for the area mention center hall Colonials and split level homes. Larger houses mean more rooms for water to reach, and that changes how a job is planned.

Courts, cul-de-sacs and getting equipment to the door

Several Old Canterbury streets are short courts and cul-de-sacs. A turnaround may leave little room for a truck, hoses and drying equipment at the same time.

When you call, tell us where a vehicle can stand and whether neighbors usually park on the street. That small detail speeds up setup and keeps hoses off busy lawns.

Common causes in larger family homes

A bigger house has more bathrooms, more supply lines and often a second-floor laundry. A failed washing machine hose or a leaking shower pan can soak a ceiling and the room below it.

See appliance leak water damage for hose and dishwasher failures, and kitchen water damage restoration for leaks under sinks and around refrigerators.

Hardwood floors are common in homes of this size. Boards that cup or lift after a leak need quick attention, and hardwood floor water damage repair describes what can be saved.

Before the crew arrives at your Old Canterbury home

Do these safety steps only if the space is dry enough to reach safely.

  • Shut the main water valve, or the valve at the fixture that failed.
  • Keep everyone out of rooms where water reaches outlets or the panel.
  • Move rugs, shoes and small furniture off wet floors.
  • Open nothing that could spread water to dry rooms, such as a drain cover.
  • Photograph damage and the failed part before anything is thrown away.

Permits and who to ask

Old Canterbury is inside the Village of Rockville Centre, so permit questions go to the village Building Department at 110 Maple Avenue. Ask before any repair that involves walls, plumbing or wiring.

Flood insurance maps for the village are kept on file with that same office. A homeowner who is unsure of a flood zone can ask there instead of guessing.
