Kitchen Water Damage Restoration in Rockville Centre, NY

Kitchens hold more water sources than any other room. We trace the leak and dry cabinets, floors and walls before they swell.

Call (516) 845-9582See all services

Kitchen water damage restoration in Rockville Centre covers cabinets, flooring, subfloor and walls soaked by a sink supply line, dishwasher, refrigerator line or a pipe in the wall. A kitchen also carries more stored goods than most rooms, so the damage reaches pantry items, appliances and finishes.

Shut off the water at the valve under the sink or at the main, and call our 24/7 line. We handle the drying, the demolition of what cannot be saved and a plan for rebuilding.

Where kitchen leaks start

The sink base is the first place to look. Supply hoses, drain connections and the garbage disposal all sit there, and a slow drip can soak the cabinet floor for months.

Dishwashers leak at the door gasket, the inlet valve and the drain hose. Refrigerators with ice makers leak from a loose or cracked supply line behind the unit.

What gets damaged

Particleboard cabinets swell and delaminate when wet, and often cannot be saved. Solid wood and plywood cabinets dry better when caught early.

Flooring under cabinets and appliances holds water. Laminate and vinyl plank can buckle, and tile can look fine while the subfloor below is wet.

How we restore a kitchen

  1. Stop the source and extract standing water.
  2. Remove toe-kicks and, if needed, lift flooring to reach the subfloor.
  3. Use meters and thermal imaging to find the wet zone behind cabinets.
  4. Dry the cavity with air movers and dehumidification.
  5. Replace materials that will not recover, then hand the room back for finish work.

If the cause was a failed appliance, appliance leak water damage explains the checks on hoses and valves. If the water spread into a floor below, see hardwood floor water damage repair for what happens to wood floors.

Kitchens in Rockville Centre homes

Many older houses near the village center have kitchens that were remodeled over the decades, with newer cabinets over older plumbing. The supply lines behind a remodeled wall may be older than the kitchen itself.

A kitchen remodel in the village needs a permit from the building department at (516) 678-9247, and contractors need a Nassau County Home Improvement License. Ask for both before you hire for the rebuild.

Cabinets, toe kicks and the space behind them

In a kitchen, the water you see is a fraction of what is there. Cabinets sit on a toe kick, and the cavity behind it traps moisture. Particleboard cabinet bases swell when wet and rarely recover.

To dry the space, a crew often removes the toe kick and drills small vent holes or lifts the base. Air movers then push dry air under the cabinets. This avoids pulling out the whole kitchen when it can be dried in place.

Check the dishwasher, the sink supply lines and the refrigerator line. Many kitchen leaks start there.

Standards behind the work

The industry reference for this kind of work is ANSI/IICRC S500, the standard for professional water damage restoration. Whoever you hire, ask how it applies to your job and ask to see the moisture readings that show the structure is dry.
