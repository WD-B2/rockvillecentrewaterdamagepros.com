Privacy Policy

How we handle information from calls and website visits.

This policy explains what information Rockville Centre Water Damage Pros collects when you visit this website or call our number, and how it is used. It was last updated on November 13, 2026.

Information we collect

This site does not use forms. When you call, we may collect your phone number, your address and details about the water damage so that we can respond. Our web server may log technical data such as your IP address and browser type.

Call recording

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. Tell the person who answers if you do not want the call recorded.

How we use information

We use it to respond to your request, schedule and perform work and improve our service. We do not sell personal information.

Cookies and analytics

This site may use basic analytics to understand traffic. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

Third parties

Pages may embed a Google map. Google handles data from that embed under its own policy.

Contact

For privacy questions, write to contact@rockvillecentrewaterdamagepros.com or call (516) 845-9582.
